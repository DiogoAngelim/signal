# Non-Financial Domain Examples

Discovery Intelligence is domain-neutral. Each domain maps its own records into the same five arrays.

## Weather

- Discovery: a newly detected storm-cell pattern
- Decision: ACT alerts, WAIT monitoring, REJECT no alert, RESTRICT limited-region alert
- Outcome: actual damage, false alarm rate, avoided exposure
- Restriction: confidence gate, sensor freshness gate, population-impact gate
- Institutional asset: a verified warning policy for a recurring storm pattern

## Logistics

- Discovery: a recurring port-delay signal
- Decision: ACT reroute, WAIT hold shipment, REJECT normal route, RESTRICT partial reroute
- Outcome: delivery time, holding cost, avoided delay
- Restriction: capacity gate, customs-readiness gate, carrier-trust gate
- Institutional asset: a standard rerouting playbook

## Healthcare Operations

- Discovery: a staffing-risk pattern
- Decision: ACT add staff, WAIT monitor, REJECT no change, RESTRICT partial shift adjustment
- Outcome: wait times, adverse events, overtime cost
- Restriction: staffing policy gate, acuity threshold, compliance gate
- Institutional asset: a unit-level staffing standard

## Fraud Detection

- Discovery: a new suspicious behavior cluster
- Decision: ACT block, WAIT review, REJECT allow, RESTRICT step-up verification
- Outcome: confirmed fraud, customer friction, avoided loss
- Restriction: false-positive gate, trust gate, manual-review gate
- Institutional asset: a verified fraud policy or reusable detection standard
