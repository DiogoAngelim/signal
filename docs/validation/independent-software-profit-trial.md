# Signal independent randomized software trial — exposure and net-profit accounting

**Study status:** software-only research infrastructure. No Workbench dependency. **No participant enrollment, real partner, approved trial, intervention, cash-ledger source, prospective software assignment, or observed causal Signal net-profit effect exists yet.**

## Why this is the missing step

Signal's earlier randomized marketing/earnings tests show some beneficial *actions*, but the historical experiments assigned those actions rather than the **use of Signal**. A software-level profit claim requires genuinely random allocation of eligible independent commercial units into two otherwise equivalent workflows:

- **Control:** independently specified conventional expected-net-profit optimizer (with the same legal actions, budget, human approvals, evidence it is entitled to request, and operational safeguards).
- **Treatment:** same workflow plus **actual Signal advice**, including the genuine, unmodified native `evaluateDecision` permission gate. A blocked or partial-scale proposal stays `wait`; it is never inflated to a full action or executed automatically.

Both policy proposals and their **real input provenance** must be created before assignment and locked into the pre-outcome manifest. Meaningful disagreement is necessary for action-policy improvement; incremental software value could also arise from *measured* human-time savings, information acquisition, evidence verification, execution failures avoided, or net harm reduction. Never invent an inferior baseline.

A good independent pilot target would be an existing, consenting, commercially active partner with many separable USD-revenue opportunities and complete receipt/refund/time/compute ledgers (for example, an opt-in subscription-retention workflow where staff manually approve permitted interventions). **No such partner was identified, contacted or enrolled here.** Choosing the pilot, funding any intervention or touching users requires explicit partner/operator approval and applicable consent/privacy review.

## Implemented, non-deploying financial handoff

The independent `scripts/validation/independent-software-profit-ledger.mjs` strictly joins three *separately authored* sources:

1. The existing frozen 10-field, one-unit-per-relationship assignment `manifest.csv`, generated by `prospective-cohort-assignment.mjs` with an independent HMAC secret and **externally timestamp-anchored before exposure**. It includes both pre-allocation candidate actions and exact model versions.
2. `exposures.jsonl`, exactly one record **for every randomized unit**, including explicit `not_shown` cases. Exact keys: `unit_hash,registered_trial_id,consent_at,eligible_at,delivery_status,delivered_at,delivered_arm,delivered_action,effective_action,action_at,human_approved_at,exposure_log_sha256`. Real exposure must follow assignment, show **only assigned-arm advice**, preserve all waits, and record genuine human approval before actual non-wait actions. No-show/nonadherence units stay in the **intent-to-treat** denominator.
3. `finances.jsonl`, exactly one independently finalized record per assigned unit. Exact keys:
   ```text
   unit_hash,registered_trial_id,outcome_at,statement_at,currency,
   receipt_cents,refund_cents,payment_fees_cents,fulfillment_cents,
   sales_minutes,sales_hourly_loaded_cents,
   delivery_minutes,delivery_hourly_loaded_cents,
   review_minutes,review_hourly_loaded_cents,
   compute_cents,other_software_cents,
   delay_cents,remediation_cents,harm_event,
   period_complete,receipts_reconciled,costs_reconciled,
   zero_receipts_verified,finance_export_sha256
   ```
   All monetary values are **nonnegative integer USD cents**, so zero must be explicitly attested (rather than created from missing data). The bounded implementation allows at most **1,000,000,000 cents per monetary component per unit**, **100,000 minutes per time component** and **1,000,000 cents per loaded hourly rate**; larger enterprise accounting exports need a separate reviewed decimal/BigInt ledger instead of a lossy conversion. Labor costs are derived as `round(minutes × loadedHourlyRateCents / 60)` separately for sales, delivery and review. Preallocate any applicable fixed software/compute expense under a frozen allocation rule and include it in `compute_cents` or `other_software_cents`; shadow costs incurred in both arms must also be recorded. Do not claim full profit when omitted taxes, chargebacks, capital costs or other real costs are material. `receipt_cents` must represent collected source-confirmed money; `refund_cents` is deducted **exactly once**. `period_complete=true` cannot substitute for reconciliation with independent source records.

Cost mapping into the existing prospective ITT auditor:
```text
gross_benefit_usd  = receipt_cents / 100
action_cost_usd    = (refunds + payment fees + fulfillment
                     + sales labor + delivery labor
                     + compute + other software) / 100
review_cost_usd    = review labor / 100
delay_cost_usd     = documented delay cost / 100
harm_cost_usd      = remediation cost / 100
net_profit_usd     = gross_benefit_usd
                     - action_cost_usd - review_cost_usd
                     - delay_cost_usd - harm_cost_usd
```
This is **net measured contribution under the declared cost boundary**, not necessarily GAAP accounting net income. Costs must use the same definitions in both trial arms. A personally advantageous intervention may still have **negative** economic effect if review, delay and software costs exceed its incremental receipts.

## External source cryptographic custody

The independently responsible financial custodian signs a **canonical SHA-256 manifest of all input file bytes** using a private Ed25519 key stored outside this repository. The verification public key comes separately via `--trusted-pubkey` and must be preapproved and anchored before the cohort is seen. The signature JSON has exactly:

`trial_id,signed_at,config_sha256,manifest_sha256,exposures_sha256,finances_sha256,signature_base64`

The signed payload is the UTF-8 newline-separated sequence:

```text
signal-independent-software-trial-custody-v1
<trial_id>
<signed_at>
<config_sha256>
<manifest_sha256>
<exposures_sha256>
<finances_sha256>

```
(with exactly one final newline). The signed timestamp must follow the last finalized financial source statement. This protects against post-signature changes and unintended source mixing; **it does not cryptographically establish the truth of the transactions, legal consent, the authority or independence of the key holder, or pre-outcome preregistration.** Those facts require external governance and separately timestamped source records.

The tool refuses wrong-arm advice, late consent or eligibility, missing human approval, inconsistent wait/action time, unmatched or duplicated randomized IDs, late source signing, wrong currency or incomplete outcome window, and any missing or invalid costs. It produces an exact-schema **18-column finalized CSV** for the existing `prospective-policy-trial-audit.mjs` plus a digest-and-count summary. Source data and output files remain private and may not be committed to GitHub or uploaded into public CI.

```sh

node scripts/validation/independent-software-profit-ledger.mjs \
  --config /secure/trial/frozen-config.json \
  --manifest /secure/trial/external-anchored-assignments.csv \
  --exposures /secure/trial/actual-software-exposures.jsonl \

  --trusted-pubkey /secure/trial/independent-custodian-public.pem \
  --finalized /secure/trial/finalized-private-itt.csv \
  --summary /secure/trial/private-ledger-summary.json

  --config /secure/trial/frozen-config.json \
  --manifest /secure/trial/external-anchored-assignments.csv \
```

The first command **cannot create enrollment or affect participants**. It uses write-once filesystem output (0600 private files), fails on a pre-existing path, does not print company-level records, and does not connect to a production database. Do **not** execute the second command until the externally reviewed observation window has fully matured and all costs are reconciled. Its favorable statistical gate alone never authorizes a scientific claim.

## What would make this a real causal profit proof?

The minimum go/no-go conditions are: independent authorized sponsor/partner and evidence of a relevant user population; real baseline net-USD variance and prespecified economically material minimum effect and sample size; immutable preregistration and independent assignment-key custody; actual randomized **advice exposure** with identical governance and real human approvals; every assigned unit included through the full financial horizon; independently reconciled source receipts and complete **incremental software costs**; source-key verification and external approval; paired policy disagreement when claims depend on action changes; and a statistically positive, practically material **lower confidence bound** with no unacceptable harm.

If the ideal, correctly specified conventional expected-net-profit optimizer sees identical information and action constraints, Signal cannot beat its expected optimum by mere re-labeling. Any real advantage must come from verified improved information, better calibrated forecasts, lower process costs, new decision-relevant evidence, or improved adherence—and must be paid for **net** of its own costs.

**Current verdict: SOFTWARE_EXPOSURE_TRIAL_NOT_STARTED; SIGNAL_CAUSAL_NET_PROFIT_NOT_IDENTIFIED.** Study infrastructure readiness is not efficacy proof.
