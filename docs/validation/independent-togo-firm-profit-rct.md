# Signal: independent real firm-profit randomized experiment (Togo) — source and causal-claim protocol

**Date:** 2026-11-14. **No Workbench data or integration.** **Status: dataset independently identified; individual microdata have NOT been retrieved, reviewed, analyzed or tested with Signal. No positive net-profit improvement attributable to Signal has been demonstrated.**

## Objective and non-negotiable interpretation

Test whether a strictly frozen, genuinely executed **Signal policy** produces higher **cost-adjusted firm profit** than a strong conventional optimizer on genuine, randomized historical firm treatment logs. This is a **retrospective counterfactual policy-value** experiment, **not** a randomized real-world *Signal software deployment* experiment. Even a positive policy-value estimate **cannot establish that using Signal software actually caused any past firm to earn more**: firms were randomized between training programs long before Signal existed.

The existing [actual Signal quantitative decision API](../../packages/decision/src/quantitative-decision.ts) exactly implements an expected-utility/risk-tail optimizer over caller-provided states, probabilities, and costs. If an equally informed conventional optimizer uses exactly the same objective and constraints, their recommendations must agree. Any claimed superiority must therefore come from a genuinely different, independently validated input-estimation/evidence/decision process, not simply relabeling expected-utility optimization as proprietary economic lift.

**Do not fabricate an ordinal `evaluateDecision` governance/evidence assessment from legacy business survey fields.** A separate quantitative-advice policy experiment may be tested, with the native action authority clearly classified as `NOT_VALIDATED_OR_EXECUTED`.

## Primary dataset: official Togo firm survey and published RCT

- [World Bank original survey microdata catalog, ID 2860](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/2860), DOI `10.48529/bj14-jj83`. Public research data, subject to the official Microdata Library's research-use, privacy and nonredistribution conditions; **obtain data through authorized access and never commit raw firm-level datasets into this repository**.
- [Original experimental result](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28935805/) (Campos et al., Science, 2017). A real three-arm randomized study of 1,500 firms, 500 assigned to no training, 500 traditional business training, and 500 personal-initiative training. Four follow-up surveys measured **business profits** over roughly two years. Published average profit lift for personal-initiative training is about 30–31% vs control; the traditional-training profit lift is about 11%, statistically nonsignificant.
- [Original protocol and intervention-cost accounting](https://www.leuphana.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Forschungseinrichtungen/rce/News/SOM_Togo_Revision_Aug2017.pdf): actual cost **$718 per participant invited** for traditional Business Edge training, **$756 per participant invited** for personal-initiative training, $0 direct program cost for no training. These are delivery costs paid by the implementing program, *not* the entrepreneurs' profits. Do not silently use the subsidized $10 fee as total cost.
- [World Bank public reproducibility package](https://reproducibility.worldbank.org/catalog/237) for the seven-year follow-up. It describes publicly available historical raw data, Stata code and published profit outcomes. The program's code/data distribution may need permitted access. The source gateway was not accessible for byte-level download in this session. The presence of a catalog page is **not** proof that its individual-level datasets have been obtained.
- [Independent backup trial candidate: McKenzie and Puerto 2021 Kenya market-level RCT](https://reproducibility.worldbank.org/catalog/22/study-description), with a two-stage randomized design and firm-profit outcomes. Its market-level spillovers require cluster-level treatment assignment and cluster-aware economic inference; do not treat individual firms within randomized markets as independent. [Independent second backup: Kenya mentors versus teachers RCT](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/sites/view/studies/113714) (372 firms). No Kenya microdata were downloaded or analyzed.

**Rejected as inappropriate for realized-profit proof:**
- `ShiwenZhang00/Coupon-AB-Test-Funnel` explicitly states the GitHub 12,959-record coupon dataset is **simulated**. Its purported customer dollar amounts must not be presented as retailer-observed profit.
- Starbucks/Udacity promotional response benchmark is expressly simulated, and Criteo's genuinely randomized ad dataset contains conversion/click outcomes but **no measured net financial outcome**; assigning $10 conversion value or assumed margin would create sensitivity calculations, not realized profit.

## Locked economic outcome and assumptions

1. **Primary observable:** real, author-defined follow-up **monthly firm operating profit** in an explicit actual source currency, converted to USD only under a prepublished exchange-rate method and independently verified provenance. Profit must already be net of business operating costs, not gross sales. Preserve every original randomized firm in the denominator, including nonresponse and closures. Absent survey values are missing, *never assumed zero*. A monthly snapshot of profit is **not** a measured cumulative 12- or 24-month profit stream.
2. **Economic comparison:** for the policy's selected training action `a`, we can define an explicitly **illustrative amortized net-profit run-rate** `reported_monthly_profit_usd - program_cost_usd(a)/H` at a frozen, predeclared horizon `H=24` months. This allocates the published one-time training cost across 24 months. It is **not** directly measured cumulative realized net profit, and should never be named such. Sensitivity must include H=12 and unamortized source profit; all policy cases apply the same accounting convention.
3. Genuine claims of **cumulative realized net USD** require independently observed profit cashflows across the horizon or a justified, validated interpolation/imputation plan with uncertainty—not an endline monthly-profit shortcut. The source's randomized action had costs, but a future Signal software operating cost was **not incurred**, so a retrospective policy comparison must not silently set Signal deployment cost to zero. Record break-even incremental Signal software expenses separately as a parameter, not an observed fact.
4. Train conventional models and freeze Signal rules using **baseline-only** covariates and a strictly isolated **training partition** of the original randomized firms; prohibit all post-treatment variables (revenue after treatment, profit, survey response, eventual training attendance). Persist fixed row hashing, model versions, source digest, all code SHA and training-only hyperparameters.
5. Use one locked test partition **once**, with 3-arm logging propensities reflecting original randomization; verify strata, treatment allocation, distribution shift, duplicated firms, and all missing outcomes. To prevent future-prediction leakage, neither Signal nor the comparator sees the test outcomes before policy scoring. The published aggregate treatment effects were already known when selecting this experiment, so label any retrospective confirmatory-appearing test **exploratory and source-selection-exposed**, never genuinely prospectively preregistered in 2013.
6. **Primary comparator:** strongest feasible equally informed conventional optimizer maximizing estimated cost-adjusted contribution, using the same preregistered data, action set, predictive calibration, budget, objective and risk governance. Add contextual outcome-model policy (e.g., pretrained validated doubly robust / T-learner) and global argmax as secondary competitive tests. Do not compare only to a deliberately weak no-training rule.
7. Frozen economic superiority gate: paired independent-firm bootstrap CI for difference in estimated policy value in USD per randomized firm must have lower 95% bound **above a positive, predeclared practical net-profit threshold**, with a sufficient effective sample size, coverage of original randomized arms, measured costs, no integrity or missingness gaps, and no adverse outcomes. Correct for multiple testing of multiple candidate policies and independent scientific review.
8. In **this historical RCT**, a favorable result could establish only `COUNTERFACTUAL_SIGNAL_POLICY_VALUE_ON_HISTORICAL_ACTION_RCT`; the claim `CAUSAL_DEPLOYMENT_OF_SIGNAL_SOFTWARE_INCREASES_REALIZED_NET_PROFIT` must always be **FALSE/NOT IDENTIFIED**. To establish the latter, separately randomize real consenting decision units to Signal advice vs conventional advice and observe fully reconciled *realized* net outcomes, with unmodified native permission and human approvals.

## Access/gating and current blocker

The World Bank official microdata portal requires compliance with research-only terms and forbids redistribution. No permission to access on the user's behalf or raw Stata bytes were obtained in this tool session. The download endpoints did not provide a verifiable local copy. Therefore **do not** invent a microdata checksum, feature columns, treatment labels, firm-level economic results, model scores, standard errors, confidence intervals or a dataset-wide profit estimate.

The companion `scripts/validation/independent-rct-profit-source-gate.mjs` is a local audit of **a summary of verified source contents**, not a data downloader or Signal outcome analyzer. The synthetic CI test demonstrates fail-closed classification; it never fabricates real profit evidence. Only after licensed raw input is available can a separate analysis safely compute real estimates.

**Research recommendation:** Togo is the strongest currently verified source for a cost-aware independent **profit-outcome** retrospective policy test. Kenya market-level RCT is the next genuine independent robustness source. **Neither contains historically randomized Signal software exposure.**
